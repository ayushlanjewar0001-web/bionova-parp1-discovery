# BioNova PARP1 — Data and Code Provenance

## Scientific & Technological Attribution

### Datasets
1. **Papyrus 2024 Benchmark Dataset**
   - Citation: Bequignon, O. J. M., et al. (2023). Papyrus: A large-scale curated dataset aimed at bioactivity predictions. *Journal of Cheminformatics*, 15(1), 3.
   - Zenodo Record: 10.5281/zenodo.13987985
   - Target UniProt: `P09874` (Human Poly [ADP-ribose] polymerase 1)
2. **ChEMBL Database (EMBL-EBI)**
   - Target ID: `CHEMBL3105`
   - Citation: Mendez, D., et al. (2019). ChEMBL: towards direct depiction of biological systems. *Nucleic Acids Research*, 47(D1), D930–D940.
3. **PubChem (NCBI)**
   - Citation: Kim, S., et al. (2023). PubChem 2023 update. *Nucleic Acids Research*, 51(D1), D1373–D1380.
4. **Protein Data Bank (PDB)**
   - PDB Entry: `7KK4`
   - Citation: Johannes, J. W., et al. (2021). Discovery of Clinical Candidate AZD5305: A Highly Selective PARP1 Inhibitor and Trapper. *J. Med. Chem.*, 64(19), 14498–14512.

### Software Packages & Frameworks
1. **ChemProp (v2.x)**: Heid, E., et al. (2024). Chemprop: A Machine Learning Package for Chemical Property Prediction. *J. Chem. Inf. Model.*
2. **AutoDock Vina**: Eberhardt, J., et al. (2021). AutoDock Vina 1.2.0: New Docking Methods, Expanded Force Field, and Python Bindings. *J. Chem. Inf. Model.*, 61(8), 3891–3898.
3. **Meeko**: For molecular preparation and flexible PDBQT generation (Forli Lab).
4. **RDKit**: Open-source cheminformatics toolkit (rdkit.org).
5. **ADMET-AI**: Swanson, K., et al. (2024). ADMET-AI: A machine learning tool for predicting ADMET properties. *Bioinformatics*.
